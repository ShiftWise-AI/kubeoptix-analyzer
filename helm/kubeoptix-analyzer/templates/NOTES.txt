1. Get the route URL:
   export ROUTE_HOST=$(oc get route {{ include "kubeoptix-analyzer.fullname" . }} -o jsonpath='{.spec.host}')

2. Check health endpoint:
   curl -k -sS "https://${ROUTE_HOST}/health"

3. Trigger analysis:
   curl -k -sS -X POST "https://${ROUTE_HOST}/run" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{}'

Reports are written to /app/data/reports inside the pod.
Artifacts must be available under /app/data/assessment.
